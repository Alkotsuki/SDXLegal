# Clarta — Support & FAQ

Clarta explains official letters in your language, tells you what to do and by when, helps you reply
and fill in forms, and reminds you before deadlines.

**Need help?** Write to [evoke-support@proton.me](mailto:evoke-support@proton.me?subject=Clarta%20support)
— in any language. We usually reply within 2 working days. In the app: **Settings › Contact support**.

## Using Clarta

**How do I explain a letter?**
Tap **Scan a letter**, scan every page, check the pages and tap **Explain this letter**. You can also
import a photo or PDF, or open a PDF in Clarta from Files or Mail.

**Which countries are supported?**
Letters from any country can be explained. Deadlines are calculated from the rules and public
holidays of the EU countries, the UK, the US and Canada; elsewhere dates are shown as written in the
letter. Always check a date marked **Check this** with the office.

**Can I change the explanation language?**
Yes — **Settings › Explanation language**. Re-explaining a letter you already scanned is free.

## Deadlines

**How are deadlines calculated?**
On your iPhone, from the date and rules in the letter, the delivery rules and the public holidays
where you live. Tap a deadline to see each step. Dates marked **As written** come straight from the
letter.

**Why don't I get reminders?**
Check **Settings › Reminders**. If notifications are off in iOS, turn them on in **iOS Settings ›
Notifications › Clarta**.

## Privacy

**What leaves my phone?**
Only the text with your personal details hidden, and page images with those details blacked out —
and only if you allowed cloud explanations. OpenAI processes it in the EU and doesn't store it.
Your Wallet is never sent anywhere. See the [Privacy Policy](/SDXLegal/clarta/privacy/).

**How do I delete my data?**
**Settings › Privacy & security › Delete all data**. Your purchased letters are kept.

## Letters & payment

**What uses a letter?**
Explaining a letter (up to 10 pages) and starting a form guide. Questions, replies, re-explaining,
translations, reminders and the Wallet are free. A failed or cancelled explanation never uses a letter.

**How do I restore purchases?**
**Settings › Restore purchases**, or on the paywall.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
**iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Clarta**, or **Settings › Manage** in Clarta.
